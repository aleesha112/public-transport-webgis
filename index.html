TwinCity Transit Atlas

Public Transport Web GIS · Rawalpindi & Islamabad, Pakistan

Study Area

Rawalpindi and Islamabad form Pakistan's twin-city region, linked by the Rawalpindi–Islamabad Metro Bus, a 22.5 km bus rapid transit corridor running from Saddar to the Pak Secretariat. This atlas maps Metro Bus stations, bus terminals, bus stops and bus routes using live OpenStreetMap data.
